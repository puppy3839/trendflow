Propagation Theory: Observed Propagation vs Cross-Domain Presence

📡

1. Observed Propagation

Applied when chronological timestamps verify that content moved sequentially: e.g. An audio drop originates on YouTube ➔ spreads to Instagram Reels ➔ spills over to X ➔ reaches Prime Time TV News.

🌐

2. Cross-Domain Presence

Applied when a major event (e.g. IPL Auction rules or Corporate WFH debates) appears across multiple digital domains simultaneously without verifiable linear causality.

🔬

3. The R₀ Virality Threshold

When reproduction rate R₀ exceeds 1.0, peer-to-peer transmission becomes self-sustaining. In mass Indian trends like Stree 2 or #ChinTapak, R₀ reaches 4.0 - 5.0.
